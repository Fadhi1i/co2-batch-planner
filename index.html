What this plan assumes

  • Cube mass means fresh concrete mass, excluding the mould.
  • Mix proportions are mass ratios. The calculated batch contains cement, aggregates and water.
  • The recipe does not include SCMs or admixtures in this first version.
  • Aggregate moisture and absorption corrections are not included. Water is converted at approximately 1 kg/L.
  • All mixing water is carbonated, and its added CO₂ concentration is retained until mixing.
  • Operating estimates use the supplied 30, 40 and 50 Hz test data. Targets outside the tested concentration range are flagged rather than extrapolated.
  • Added CO₂ delivered through water and CO₂ mineralized in concrete are separate quantities.
